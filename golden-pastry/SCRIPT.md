# Golden Pastry — promo script (A + B), 9:16

Format: 1080×1920, 30 fps, about 50–55 s. Two films: English and Romanian, same picture.
Style: the Lira Pay "liquid" film — one new beat every 1–2 s, glossy clay shapes,
squash and stretch, kinetic type, real app screens shown as they are (never redrawn).
Brand: Golden Pastry gold `#E9A23B`, caramel `#B5541C`, espresso `#2B1A12`, cream `#FBF3E6`.

Every claim below is on goldenpastry.ro: bake-off goes "into the oven straight from the
freezer, no thawing, no baker"; finished product is "baked or fried by us, individually
packed — thaw and sell, or warm on the spot"; delivery 24–48 h; −18 °C unbroken cold chain;
trial pack = 3 of each product.
